# Etsy flat-lay sellers — pilot list

Pilot of 236 Etsy sellers whose listings show garments off-body (flat lay, hanger, or dress form), filtered to high-income markets.

**Distribution:** US=214, CA=7, UK=4, HI=11

**Tier legend:** **US** = United States, **CA** = Canada, **UK** = United Kingdom, **HI** = other high-income (Western Europe, Australia/NZ, Japan, etc.).

Captured 2026-05-05. Sellers were sourced by searching Etsy across many garment-category queries (vintage dress, blouse, skirt, pants, jacket, sweater, coat, shirt, denim, gown, cardigan, suit, etc.) with the `item_country=US` filter, then visually classifying primary thumbnails for off-body presentation, deduping to unique sellers, fetching each shop page to read seller location, and filtering to high-income countries (excluding India and other lower-income markets per project scope).

Note: 88 candidate sellers had hidden shop locations and could not be classified — they are not included.
